An AI-powered chatbot for **disaster preparedness and medical emergency guidance** using Google Gemini 2.5 Flash.

## 📌 Project Overview

Disaster Shield AI is a Generative AI-based assistant designed to provide quick and reliable information during emergency situations.

The chatbot helps users with:
- Disaster preparedness guidance
- Emergency response information
- Basic medical emergency guidance

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

During disasters and emergencies, people often struggle to access reliable information quickly. Searching through long emergency manuals can be difficult, and incorrect information during critical situations can increase risks.

This project aims to provide users with simple, conversational, and accessible emergency guidance.
